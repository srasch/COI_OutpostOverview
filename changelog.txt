v0.2.0 | 2026-11-07
* New: automatic pause / resume per mine. Click the gear button next to a mine's pause button to open storage-style sliders: the red slider pauses the mine once its storage reaches that level, the green slider resumes it when the storage drops below that level (5 % steps)
* A small hint like "30-90 %" next to the mine name shows which mines have automatic limits set
* Automatic pause / resume also works while the window is closed, and the limits are remembered per game
* Pausing or resuming a mine by hand is respected until the storage crosses the next limit

v0.1.0 | 2026-11-07
* Initial release: a window listing all your repaired world map mines, opened with the toolbar button next to the world map or with F8 (rebindable in Settings > Controls)
* One compact line per mine: product icon, name, storage fill level (yellow at 90 %, red when full), remaining reserve, estimated years left and status
* Sort by mine name, storage, reserve or years left with a click on the column header, or drag the mines into your own order
* Pause or resume each mine directly from the list
* Hide the reserve and status columns to make the window narrower; with the status column hidden, the mine name shows the status by color
* The window can be moved and pinned; sorting, your own order and the column settings are remembered per game
* The toolbar button appears once you own a world mine; F8 always works
* English and German texts
